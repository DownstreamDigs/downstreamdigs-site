About

Started in the river, not a classroom.

Downstream Digs started as two people spending every free weekend wrist-deep in Florida's rivers, and it turned into guiding others through the same water. We're based out of Jupiter, FL, running private trips on the Peace River.

Every trip is hands-in-the-water. You'll learn where fossils collect, how to work a sifter without missing anything, and what you're actually holding once you find it.
